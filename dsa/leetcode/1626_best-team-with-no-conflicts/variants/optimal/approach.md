## General
Given You are the manager of a basketball team. For the upcoming tournament, you want to choose the team with the highest overall score. The score of the team is the **sum** of scores of all the players in the team, the algorithm executes a single-pass linear scan through input elements.

## Complexity detail
- **Time Complexity**: $O(n\log n)$ — Operation count bound.
- **Space Complexity**: $O(n)$ — Auxiliary memory allocation bound.
