## General
Given You have `n` bags numbered from `0` to $n - 1$. You are given two **0-indexed** integer arrays `capacity` and `rocks`. The $$i^{\text{th}}$$ bag can hold a maximum of $\text{capacity}[i]$ rocks and currently contains ..., the algorithm executes a single-pass linear scan through input elements.

## Complexity detail
- **Time Complexity**: $O(n log n)$ — Operation count bound.
- **Space Complexity**: $O(n)$ — Auxiliary memory allocation bound.
