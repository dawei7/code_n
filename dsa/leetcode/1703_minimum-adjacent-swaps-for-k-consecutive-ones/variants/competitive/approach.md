## General
Given an integer array, `nums`, and an integer `k`. `nums` comprises of only `0`'s and `1`'s. In one move, you can choose two **adjacent** indices and swap their values, the algorithm executes a single-pass linear scan through input elements.

## Complexity detail
- **Time Complexity**: $O(n)$ — Operation count bound.
- **Space Complexity**: $O(m)$ — Auxiliary memory allocation bound.
