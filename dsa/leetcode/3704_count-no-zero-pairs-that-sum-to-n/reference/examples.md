## Examples

**Example 1**

- Input: `n = 2`
- Output: `1`
- Explanation: The sole qualifying pair is `(1, 1)`.

**Example 2**

- Input: `n = 3`
- Output: `2`
- Explanation: The two ordered pairs are `(1, 2)` and `(2, 1)`.

**Example 3**

- Input: `n = 11`
- Output: `8`
- Explanation: The qualifying pairs are `(2, 9)`, `(3, 8)`, `(4, 7)`, `(5, 6)`, `(6, 5)`, `(7, 4)`, `(8, 3)`, and `(9, 2)`. The remaining decompositions `(1, 10)` and `(10, 1)` are invalid because the decimal representation of `10` contains `0`.
