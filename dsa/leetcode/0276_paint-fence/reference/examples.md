## Examples

**Example 1**

- Input: `n = 3, k = 2`
- Output: `6`
- Explanation: Calling the two colors `R` and `G`, the six permitted arrangements are shown below.

```text
R R G    R G R    G R R
G G R    G R G    R G G
```

The arrangements `R R R` and `G G G` are invalid because all three consecutive posts would have one color.
