## Examples

**Example 1**

- Input: `nums = [1,4,2,5]`
- Output: `8`
- Explanation: Choose `a = 4`, `b = 5`, and `c = 1`. The expression becomes `4 + 5 - 1 = 8`, which is the maximum possible value.

**Example 2**

- Input: `nums = [-2,0,5,-2,4]`
- Output: `11`
- Explanation: Choose `a = 5`, `b = 4`, and `c = -2`. The expression becomes `5 + 4 - (-2) = 11`, which is the maximum possible value.
