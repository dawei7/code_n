## Examples

**Example 1**

- Input: `prices = [7, 1, 5, 3, 6, 4]`
- Output: `5`
- Explanation: Buying on day 2 for `1` and selling on day 5 for `6` yields `6 - 1 = 5`. Day 1 cannot be the sale paired with day 2 because the purchase must occur first.

**Example 2**

- Input: `prices = [7, 6, 4, 3, 1]`
- Output: `0`
- Explanation: Every later price is lower, so the best choice is to make no transaction and keep profit at `0`.
