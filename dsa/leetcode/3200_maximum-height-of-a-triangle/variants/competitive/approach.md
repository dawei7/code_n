## General
Given two integers `red` and `blue` representing the count of red and blue colored balls. You have to arrange these balls to form a triangle such that the 1^st row will have 1 ball, the 2^nd row will have 2 balls, the 3^rd ..., the algorithm solves **Maximum Height of a Triangle** directly.

## Complexity detail
- **Time Complexity**: $O(1)$ — Operation count bound.
- **Space Complexity**: $O(1)$ — Auxiliary memory allocation bound.
