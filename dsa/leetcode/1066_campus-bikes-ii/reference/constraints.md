## Constraints

- $n = \lvert\texttt{workers}\rvert$
- $m = \lvert\texttt{bikes}\rvert$
- $1 \le n \le m \le 10$
- Every `workers[i]` contains exactly two coordinates.
- Every `bikes[i]` contains exactly two coordinates.
- $0 \le \texttt{workers[i][0]},\texttt{workers[i][1]},\texttt{bikes[i][0]},\texttt{bikes[i][1]} < 1000$
- All worker and bike locations are unique across both arrays.
