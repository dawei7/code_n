## Examples

**Example 1**

- **Input:** `s = "havefunonleetcode", k = 5`
- **Output:** `6`
- **Explanation:** The six qualifying substrings are `"havef"`, `"avefu"`, `"vefun"`, `"efuno"`, `"etcod"`, and `"tcode"`.

**Example 2**

- **Input:** `s = "home", k = 5`
- **Output:** `0`
- **Explanation:** Here `k` is greater than the length of `s`, so no substring of the requested length can be formed.
