## Examples

**Example 1**

- Input: `nums = [2,4,6,8,10]`
- Output: `7`
- Explanation: The seven arithmetic subsequences are `[2,4,6]`, `[4,6,8]`, `[6,8,10]`, `[2,4,6,8]`, `[4,6,8,10]`, `[2,4,6,8,10]`, and `[2,6,10]`.

**Example 2**

- Input: `nums = [7,7,7,7,7]`
- Output: `16`
- Explanation: Every subsequence of this array with at least three selected positions is arithmetic because every consecutive difference is zero.
