## General
Given a string `s` consisting of lowercase English letters, and an integer `k`. Your task is to *convert* the string into an integer by a special process, and then *transform* it by summing its digits repeatedly `k` times. ..., the algorithm solves **Sum of Digits of String After Convert** directly.

## Complexity detail
- **Time Complexity**: $O(N)$ — Operation count bound.
- **Space Complexity**: $O(1)$ — Auxiliary memory allocation bound.
