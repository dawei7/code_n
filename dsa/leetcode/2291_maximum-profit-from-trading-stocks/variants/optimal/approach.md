## General
Given two **0-indexed** integer arrays of the same length `present` and `future` where $\text{present}[i]$ is the current price of the $$i^{\text{th}}$$ stock and $\text{future}[i]$ is the price of the $$i^{\text{th}}$$ sto..., the algorithm executes a single-pass linear scan through input elements.

## Complexity detail
- **Time Complexity**: $O(nB)$ — Operation count bound.
- **Space Complexity**: $O(B)$ — Auxiliary memory allocation bound.
