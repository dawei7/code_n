## Examples

**Example 1**

- Input: `nums1 = [1,7,11], nums2 = [2,4,6], k = 3`
- Output: `[[1,2],[1,4],[1,6]]`
- Explanation: In increasing-sum order the pairs are `[1,2]`, `[1,4]`, `[1,6]`, `[7,2]`, `[7,4]`, `[11,2]`, `[7,6]`, `[11,4]`, and `[11,6]`; the first three are returned.

**Example 2**

- Input: `nums1 = [1,1,2], nums2 = [1,2,3], k = 2`
- Output: `[[1,1],[1,1]]`
- Explanation: The ordered sequence is `[1,1]`, `[1,1]`, `[1,2]`, `[2,1]`, `[1,2]`, `[2,2]`, `[1,3]`, `[1,3]`, `[2,3]`; the first two pairs are selected.
