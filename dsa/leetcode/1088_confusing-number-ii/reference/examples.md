## Examples

**Example 1**

- **Input:** `n = 20`
- **Output:** `6`
- **Explanation:** The confusing numbers are `[6,9,10,16,18,19]`. Their rotations are `9`, `6`, `01` (the integer `1`), `91`, `81`, and `61`, respectively.

**Example 2**

- **Input:** `n = 100`
- **Output:** `19`
- **Explanation:** The complete list is `[6,9,10,16,18,19,60,61,66,68,80,81,86,89,90,91,98,99,100]`.
