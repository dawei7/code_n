## Examples

**Example 1**

- **Input:** `ships = [[1,1],[2,2],[3,3],[5,5]], topRight = [4,4], bottomLeft = [0,0]`
- **Output:** `3`
- **Explanation:** The inclusive rectangle from `[0,0]` through `[4,4]` contains the ships at `[1,1]`, `[2,2]`, and `[3,3]`; `[5,5]` is outside it.

**Example 2**

- **Input:** `ans = [[1,1],[2,2],[3,3]], topRight = [1000,1000], bottomLeft = [0,0]`
- **Output:** `3`
