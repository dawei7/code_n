## Examples

**Example 1**

- Input: `s = "abcdeca", k = 2`
- Output: `true`
- Explanation: Removing `b` and `e` leaves the palindrome `acdca`.

**Example 2**

- Input: `s = "abbababa", k = 1`
- Output: `true`

### Additional Examples

**Insufficient budget**

- Input: `s = "abc", k = 1`
- Output: `false`

At least two characters must be removed to leave a palindrome.
