## General
Given a collection of candidate numbers (`candidates`) and a target number (`target`), find all unique combinations in `candidates` where the candidate numbers sum to `target`, the algorithm executes depth-first search (DFS) recursion to explore valid decision branches.

## Complexity detail
- **Time Complexity**: $O(n \cdot 2^n)$ — Operation count bound.
- **Space Complexity**: $O(n)$ — Auxiliary memory allocation bound.
