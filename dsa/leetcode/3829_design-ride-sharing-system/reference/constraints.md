## Constraints

- $1 \le \texttt{riderId}, \texttt{driverId} \le 1000$
- Every `riderId` is unique among riders and is added at most once.
- Every `driverId` is unique among drivers and is added at most once.
- Across `addRider`, `addDriver`, `matchDriverWithRider`, and `cancelRider`, at most $1000$ calls are made in total.
