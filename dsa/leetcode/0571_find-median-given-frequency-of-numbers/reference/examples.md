## Examples

**Example 1**

- **Input:** `Numbers = [[0,7],[1,1],[2,3],[3,1]]`

| num | frequency |
|---:|---:|
| 0 | 7 |
| 1 | 1 |
| 2 | 3 |
| 3 | 1 |

- **Output:** `[[0.0]]`

| median |
|---:|
| 0.0 |

- **Explanation:** Decompression produces `[0,0,0,0,0,0,0,1,2,2,2,3]`. The collection has twelve values, and both central positions contain `0`, so their average is `(0 + 0) / 2 = 0` and the reported value is `0.0`.
