## Constraints

- $1 \le \text{equations.length} \le 100$.
- $\text{equations}[i].\text{length} == 2$.
- $1 \le A_i.\text{length}, B_i.\text{length} \le 5$.
- $A_i$ and $B_i$ consist of lowercase English letters.
- $\text{values.length} == \text{equations.length}$.
- $0.0 < \text{values}[i] \le 10.0$.
- `values[i]` has at most two decimal places.
