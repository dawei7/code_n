## General
Given the `root` of a **binary tree** that consists of exactly `3` nodes: the root, its left child, and its right child, the algorithm executes a two-pointer approach to shrink boundaries or maintain a sliding window. It utilizes binary tree node references (`val`, `left`, `right`) to traverse structural hierarchies.

## Complexity detail
- **Time Complexity**: $O(1)$ — Operation count bound.
- **Space Complexity**: $O(1)$ — Auxiliary memory allocation bound.
