## Examples

**Example 1**

- Input: `sides = [3,4,5]`
- Output: `[36.86990,53.13010,90.00000]`
- Explanation: These lengths form a right triangle. Its internal angles are approximately $36.869897646^\circ$, $53.130102354^\circ$, and $90^\circ$, already listed from smallest to largest.

**Example 2**

- Input: `sides = [2,4,2]`
- Output: `[]`
- Explanation: The two shorter lengths add to the longest length instead of exceeding it, so the shape is degenerate and has zero area rather than being a valid triangle.
