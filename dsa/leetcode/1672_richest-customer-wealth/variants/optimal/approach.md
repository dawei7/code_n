## General
Given an `m x n` integer grid `accounts` where $\text{accounts}[i][j]$ is the amount of money the $i​​​​​^​​​​​​th​​​​$ customer has in the $j​​​​​^​​​​​​th$​​​​ bank. Return* the **wealth** that the richest customer has.*, the algorithm executes a single-pass linear scan through input elements.

## Complexity detail
- **Time Complexity**: $O(S)$ — Operation count bound.
- **Space Complexity**: $O(1)$ — Auxiliary memory allocation bound.
