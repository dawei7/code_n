## Examples

**Example 1**

- Input: `n = 122`
- Output: `5`
- Explanation:
  - Digit `1` occurs once, so it contributes `1 * 1 = 1`.
  - Digit `2` occurs twice, so it contributes `2 * 2 = 4`.
  - The total score is `1 + 4 = 5`.

**Example 2**

- Input: `n = 101`
- Output: `2`
- Explanation:
  - Digit `0` occurs once and contributes `0 * 1 = 0`.
  - Digit `1` occurs twice and contributes `1 * 2 = 2`.
  - Adding the two contributions gives a score of `2`.
