## General
Given an integer array `height` of length `n`. There are `n` vertical lines drawn such that the two endpoints of the $$i^{\text{th}}$$ line are `(i, 0)` and $(i, \text{height}[i])$, the algorithm executes a two-pointer approach to shrink boundaries or maintain a sliding window.

## Complexity detail
- **Time Complexity**: $O(n)$ — Operation count bound.
- **Space Complexity**: $O(1)$ — Auxiliary memory allocation bound.
