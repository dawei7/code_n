## Examples

**Example 1**

- Input: `Players = [[15,1],[25,1],[30,1],[45,1],[10,2],[35,2],[50,2],[20,3],[40,3]], Matches = [[1,15,45,3,0],[2,30,25,1,2],[3,30,15,2,0],[4,40,20,5,2],[5,35,50,1,1]]`
- Output: `[[1,15],[2,35],[3,40]]`

`Players`

| player_id | group_id |
|---:|---:|
| 15 | 1 |
| 25 | 1 |
| 30 | 1 |
| 45 | 1 |
| 10 | 2 |
| 35 | 2 |
| 50 | 2 |
| 20 | 3 |
| 40 | 3 |

`Matches`

| match_id | first_player | second_player | first_score | second_score |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 1 | 15 | 45 | 3 | 0 |
| 2 | 30 | 25 | 1 | 2 |
| 3 | 30 | 15 | 2 | 0 |
| 4 | 40 | 20 | 5 | 2 |
| 5 | 35 | 50 | 1 | 1 |

Result:

| group_id | player_id |
|---:|---:|
| 1 | 15 |
| 2 | 35 |
| 3 | 40 |
