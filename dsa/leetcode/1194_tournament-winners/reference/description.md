## Description

Every player belongs to a tournament group, and every recorded match gives the points earned by its two same-group participants. A player's total is the sum of all points that player scored across every match, whether listed as the first or second participant.

Find one winner for each group. The winner has the greatest total score within that group; if several players share the maximum, choose the one with the lowest `player_id`. Return every group's winning identifier, with no required row order.
