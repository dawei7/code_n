## General
Given Your country has $10^{9}$ lakes. Initially, all the lakes are empty, but when it rains over the $$n^{\text{th}}$$ lake, the $$n^{\text{th}}$$ lake becomes full of water. If it rains over a lake that is **full of water..., the algorithm executes a single-pass linear scan through input elements. It utilizes a hash map (`dict`) to store element values and their indices/frequencies for $O(1)$ fast access.

## Complexity detail
- **Time Complexity**: $O(N \log N)$ — Operation count bound.
- **Space Complexity**: $O(N)$ — Auxiliary memory allocation bound.
