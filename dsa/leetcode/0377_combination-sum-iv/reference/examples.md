## Examples

**Example 1**

- Input: `nums = [1,2,3], target = 4`
- Output: `7`
- Explanation: The seven ordered combinations are `(1,1,1,1)`, `(1,1,2)`, `(1,2,1)`, `(1,3)`, `(2,1,1)`, `(2,2)`, and `(3,1)`. Sequences with different orders are counted separately.

**Example 2**

- Input: `nums = [9], target = 3`
- Output: `0`
