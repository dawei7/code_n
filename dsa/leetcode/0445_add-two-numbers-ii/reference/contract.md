## Function Contract

**Inputs**

- `l1`: The head of the first non-empty `ListNode` chain, with its most significant digit first.
- `l2`: The head of the second non-empty `ListNode` chain, with its most significant digit first.

**Return value**

- Return the head of a `ListNode` chain whose digits represent the sum in most-significant-first order.

The standalone app defines a minimal local equivalent of LeetCode's injected `ListNode` model and uses the same linked-list interface.
