## Examples

**Example 1**

| id | name | salary | managerId |

| 1 | Joe | 70000 | 3 |
| 2 | Henry | 80000 | 4 |
| 3 | Sam | 60000 | null |
| 4 | Max | 90000 | null |

- Output: `Employee = ["Joe"]`

| Employee |
|---|
| Joe |

- Explanation: Joe is the only employee whose salary exceeds that of his manager.
