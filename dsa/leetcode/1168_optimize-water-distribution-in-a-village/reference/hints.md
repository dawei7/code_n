## Hints

1. Explore a graph formulation of the village and its choices.
2. Represent each house as a vertex and each pipe offer as a weighted edge.
3. Determine how building a well directly at a house can also be expressed as an edge choice.
4. Introduce one extra water-source vertex, and connect it to each house with an edge whose weight is that house's well cost.
5. The resulting task is to find a minimum spanning tree of the augmented graph.
