## General
Given There is an intersection of two roads. First road is road A where cars travel from North to South in direction 1 and from South to North in direction 2. Second road is road B where cars travel from West to East in dir..., the algorithm solves **Traffic Light Controlled Intersection** directly.

## Complexity detail
- **Time Complexity**: $O(1)$ — Operation count bound.
- **Space Complexity**: $O(1)$ — Auxiliary memory allocation bound.
