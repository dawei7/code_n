## General
Given an integer `finalSum`. Split it into a sum of a **maximum** number of **unique** positive even integers, the algorithm solves **Maximum Split of Positive Even Integers** directly. Edge case handling: applies modulo arithmetic to prevent integer overflow.

## Complexity detail
- **Time Complexity**: $O(\sqrt{S})$ — Operation count bound.
- **Space Complexity**: $O(\sqrt{S})$ — Auxiliary memory allocation bound.
