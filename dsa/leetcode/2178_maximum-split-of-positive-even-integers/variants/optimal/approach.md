## General
Given an integer `finalSum`. Split it into a sum of a **maximum** number of **unique** positive even integers, the algorithm solves **Maximum Split of Positive Even Integers** directly. Key operations include bitwise operators (`&`, `|`, `^`, `<<`, `>>`) for fast bitmask state updates.

## Complexity detail
- **Time Complexity**: $O(\sqrt{S})$ — Operation count bound.
- **Space Complexity**: $O(\sqrt{S})$ — Auxiliary memory allocation bound.
