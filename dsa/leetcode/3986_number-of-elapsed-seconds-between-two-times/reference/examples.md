## Examples

**Example 1**

- Input: `startTime = "01:00:00", endTime = "01:00:25"`
- Output: `25`
- **Explanation:** The ending time is exactly 25 seconds after the starting time.

**Example 2**

- Input: `startTime = "12:34:56", endTime = "13:00:00"`
- Output: `1504`
- **Explanation:** The interval lasts 25 minutes and 4 seconds. Converting that duration gives `25 * 60 + 4 = 1504` seconds.
