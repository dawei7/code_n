## Description

Compute the tournament score of every team after all recorded matches. Award points independently for each match:

- A win earns the team three points because it scored more goals than its opponent.
- A draw earns the team one point because both teams scored the same number of goals.
- A loss earns the team no points because it scored fewer goals than its opponent.

Accumulate a team's points across both host and guest appearances. Every tournament team belongs in the final standings, including a team whose total is zero.
