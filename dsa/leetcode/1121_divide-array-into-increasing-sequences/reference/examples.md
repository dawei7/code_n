## Examples

**Example 1**

- **Input:** `nums = [1,2,2,3,3,4,4], k = 3`
- **Output:** `true`
- **Explanation:** Divide the array into `[1,2,3,4]` and `[2,3,4]`. Both subsequences are increasing, and their lengths are at least `3`.

**Example 2**

- **Input:** `nums = [5,6,6,7,8], k = 3`
- **Output:** `false`
- **Explanation:** No division of the array satisfies all of the required conditions.
