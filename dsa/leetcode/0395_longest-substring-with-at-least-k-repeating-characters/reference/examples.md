## Examples

**Example 1**

- Input: `s = "aaabb", k = 3`
- Output: `3`
- Explanation: The longest qualifying substring is `"aaa"`, in which `a` occurs three times.

**Example 2**

- Input: `s = "ababbc", k = 2`
- Output: `5`
- Explanation: The longest qualifying substring is `"ababb"`; it contains two `a` characters and three `b` characters.
