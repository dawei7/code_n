## Examples

**Example 1**

- Input: `s = "a"`
- Output: `1`
- **Explanation:** The only substring of `s` that occurs in `base` is `"a"`.

**Example 2**

- Input: `s = "cac"`
- Output: `2`
- **Explanation:** Exactly two substrings occur in `base`: `"a"` and `"c"`.

**Example 3**

- Input: `s = "zab"`
- Output: `6`
- **Explanation:** The six qualifying substrings are `"z"`, `"a"`, `"b"`, `"za"`, `"ab"`, and `"zab"`.
