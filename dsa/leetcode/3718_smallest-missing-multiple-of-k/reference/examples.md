## Examples

**Example 1**

- Input: `nums = [8,2,3,4,6], k = 2`
- Output: `10`
- Explanation: The positive multiples of `2` begin `2, 4, 6, 8, 10, 12, ...`. The array contains the first four of them, making `10` the earliest missing one.

**Example 2**

- Input: `nums = [1,4,7,10,15], k = 5`
- Output: `5`
- Explanation: The positive multiples of `5` begin `5, 10, 15, 20, ...`. Although `10` and `15` occur in the array, `5` does not, so it is the smallest missing multiple.
