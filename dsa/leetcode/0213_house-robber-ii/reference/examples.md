## Examples

**Example 1**

- Input: `nums = [2,3,2]`
- Output: `3`
- Explanation: Houses `1` and `3` cannot both be robbed because their positions are adjacent in the circle.

**Example 2**

- Input: `nums = [1,2,3,1]`
- Output: `4`
- Explanation: Rob houses `1` and `3` for a total of `1 + 3 = 4`.

**Example 3**

- Input: `nums = [1,2,3]`
- Output: `3`
