## General
Given an integer `n`, representing `n` light bulbs arranged in a line and indexed from 0 to $n - 1$, the algorithm executes a two-pointer approach to shrink boundaries or maintain a sliding window.

## Complexity detail
- **Time Complexity**: $O(m log m)$ — Operation count bound.
- **Space Complexity**: $O(m)$ — Auxiliary memory allocation bound.
