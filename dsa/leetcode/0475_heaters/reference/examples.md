## Examples

**Example 1**

- Input: `houses = [1, 2, 3], heaters = [2]`
- Output: `1`
- Explanation: The heater at position 2 reaches every listed house when its radius is 1.

**Example 2**

- Input: `houses = [1, 2, 3, 4], heaters = [1, 4]`
- Output: `1`
- Explanation: With heaters at positions 1 and 4, a common radius of 1 is enough to warm all four houses.

**Example 3**

- Input: `houses = [1, 5], heaters = [2]`
- Output: `3`
