## General
Given Winter is coming! During the contest, your first job is to design a standard heater with a fixed warm radius to warm all the houses, the algorithm executes a single-pass linear scan through input elements.

## Complexity detail
- **Time Complexity**: $O((h + t) \log(h + t))$ — Operation count bound.
- **Space Complexity**: $O(t)$ — Auxiliary memory allocation bound.
