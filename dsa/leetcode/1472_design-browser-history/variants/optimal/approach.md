## General
Given You have a **browser** of one tab where you start on the `homepage` and you can visit another `url`, get back in the history number of `steps` or move forward in the history number of `steps`, the algorithm solves **Design Browser History** directly.

## Complexity detail
- **Time Complexity**: $O(q)$ — Operation count bound.
- **Space Complexity**: $O(v)$ — Auxiliary memory allocation bound.
