## General
Given two arrays of positive integers, `boxes` and `warehouse`, representing the heights of some boxes of unit width and the heights of `n` rooms in a warehouse respectively. The warehouse's rooms are labeled from `0` to $n..., the algorithm executes a two-pointer approach to shrink boundaries or maintain a sliding window.

## Complexity detail
- **Time Complexity**: $O(B\log B + W\log W)$ — Operation count bound.
- **Space Complexity**: $O(W)$ — Auxiliary memory allocation bound.
