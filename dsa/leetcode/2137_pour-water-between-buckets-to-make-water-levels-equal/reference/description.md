## Description

You have `n` buckets, each containing a certain amount of water represented by a **0-indexed** integer array `buckets`, where `buckets[i]` is the amount of water in the `i^th` bucket. You are also given an integer `loss`.

You want to make the amount of water in all buckets equal. To achieve this, you can pour water from one bucket to another any number of times (possibly zero). However, every time you pour water, `loss` percentage of the poured water is spilled, and only the remaining water reaches the target bucket.

Return *the maximum possible amount of water in each bucket after making the amount of water in all buckets equal*. Answers within `10^-5` of the actual answer will be accepted.
