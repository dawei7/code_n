## Examples

**Example 1**

- Input: `nums = [1,2,3,4,5,6,7], k = 3`
- Output: `[5,6,7,1,2,3,4]`
- Explanation: The successive right rotations are `[7,1,2,3,4,5,6]`, then `[6,7,1,2,3,4,5]`, and finally `[5,6,7,1,2,3,4]`.

**Example 2**

- Input: `nums = [-1,-100,3,99], k = 2`
- Output: `[3,99,-1,-100]`
- Explanation: One right rotation gives `[99,-1,-100,3]`; the second gives `[3,99,-1,-100]`.
