## Constraints

- $1 \le \texttt{chunks.length} \le 10^5$
- $1 \le \texttt{chunks}[i]\texttt{.length} \le 10^5$
- The total length $C$ of all strings in `chunks` is at most $10^5$.
- Every `chunks[i]` contains only lowercase English letters, spaces, and `'-'`.
- $1 \le \texttt{queries.length} \le 10^5$
- $1 \le \texttt{queries}[i]\texttt{.length} \le 10^5$
- The total length $Q$ of all strings in `queries` is at most $10^5$.
- Every `queries[i]` contains only lowercase English letters and `'-'`.
- Every `queries[i]` is a valid word: it neither starts nor ends with `'-'` and contains no consecutive hyphens.
