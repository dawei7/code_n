## Constraints

- $n = \lvert\texttt{nums}\rvert$.
- $1 \le n \le 10^4$.
- `nums` is a permutation containing every integer in the inclusive range $[1,n]$.
- $1 \le \lvert\texttt{sequences}\rvert \le 10^4$.
- $1 \le \lvert\texttt{sequences}[i]\rvert \le 10^4$.
- $1 \le S \le 10^5$, where $S = \sum_i \lvert\texttt{sequences}[i]\rvert$.
- $1 \le \texttt{sequences}[i][j] \le n$.
- All rows of `sequences` are unique.
- Every `sequences[i]` is a subsequence of `nums`.
