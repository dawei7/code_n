## Description

You are given integer arrays `nums1` and `nums2`, with lengths `n` and `m`, and an integer `k`. Choose exactly `k` pairs of indices. The indices chosen from `nums1` must be strictly increasing, and the independently chosen indices from `nums2` must also be strictly increasing.

A selected pair `(i, j)` contributes the product `nums1[i] * nums2[j]`. The total score is the sum of the products contributed by all `k` pairs.

Return the maximum total score achievable under both ordering requirements.
