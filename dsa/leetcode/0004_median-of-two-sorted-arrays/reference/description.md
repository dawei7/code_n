## Description

Two integer arrays, `nums1` and `nums2`, are sorted in ascending order. Treat their elements as one combined sorted collection and return that collection's median.

If the combined length is odd, the median is its single middle value. If the length is even, the median is the arithmetic mean of its two middle values. The required overall running time is $O(\log(m+n))$, where $m$ and $n$ are the respective input lengths.
