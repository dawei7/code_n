## General
Given two sorted arrays `nums1` and `nums2` of size `m` and `n` respectively, return **the median** of the two sorted arrays, the algorithm solves **Median of Two Sorted Arrays** directly.

## Complexity detail
- **Time Complexity**: $O(\log(\min(m, n)))$ — Operation count bound.
- **Space Complexity**: $O(1)$ — Auxiliary memory allocation bound.
