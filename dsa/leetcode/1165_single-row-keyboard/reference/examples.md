## Examples

**Example 1**

- Input: `keyboard = "abcdefghijklmnopqrstuvwxyz", word = "cba"`
- Output: `4`
- Explanation: The finger goes from position `0` to `2` for `c`, then to `1` for `b`, and finally to `0` for `a`. The total is `2 + 1 + 1 = 4`.

**Example 2**

- Input: `keyboard = "pqrstuvwxyzabcdefghijklmno", word = "leetcode"`
- Output: `73`
