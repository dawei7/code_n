## General
Given Alice and Bob are playing a turn-based game on a field, with two lanes of flowers between them. There are `x` flowers in the first lane between Alice and Bob, and `y` flowers in the second lane between them, the algorithm solves **Alice and Bob Playing Flower Game** directly.

## Complexity detail
- **Time Complexity**: $O(1)$ — Operation count bound.
- **Space Complexity**: $O(1)$ — Auxiliary memory allocation bound.
