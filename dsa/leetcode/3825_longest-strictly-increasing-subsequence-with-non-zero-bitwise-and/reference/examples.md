## Examples

**Example 1**

- Input: `nums = [5,4,7]`
- Output: `2`
- Explanation: One longest strictly increasing subsequence is `[5, 7]`. Its bitwise AND is `5 AND 7 = 5`, which is non-zero.

**Example 2**

- Input: `nums = [2,3,6]`
- Output: `3`
- Explanation: The longest strictly increasing subsequence is `[2, 3, 6]`. Its bitwise AND is `2 AND 3 AND 6 = 2`, which is non-zero.

**Example 3**

- Input: `nums = [0,1]`
- Output: `1`
- Explanation: One longest strictly increasing subsequence is `[1]`. Its bitwise AND is `1`, which is non-zero.
