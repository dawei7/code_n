## Examples

**Example 1**

- Input: `words = ["area", "lead", "wall", "lady", "ball"]`
- Output: `[["ball", "area", "lead", "lady"], ["wall", "area", "lead", "lady"]]`
- Explanation: The result contains two word squares. Their order in the outer list is irrelevant, but the word order
  inside each square is significant.

**Example 2**

- Input: `words = ["abat", "baba", "atan", "atal"]`
- Output: `[["baba", "abat", "baba", "atal"], ["baba", "abat", "baba", "atan"]]`
- Explanation: These are the two word squares. The squares may be returned in either order, while each square must
  retain its shown row order.
