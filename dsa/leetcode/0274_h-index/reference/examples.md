## Examples

**Example 1**

- Input: `citations = [3,0,6,1,5]`
- Output: `3`
- Explanation: The five entries report that the researcher's papers received `3`, `0`, `6`, `1`, and `5` citations. Three papers have at least three citations apiece, while the other two have no more than three, so the h-index is `3`.

**Example 2**

- Input: `citations = [1,3,1]`
- Output: `1`
