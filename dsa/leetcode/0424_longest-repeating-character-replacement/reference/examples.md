## Examples

**Example 1**

- Input: `s = "ABAB", k = 2`
- Output: `4`
- Explanation: Replace both `A` characters with `B`, or replace both `B` characters with `A`, to make all four
  positions equal.

**Example 2**

- Input: `s = "AABABBA", k = 1`
- Output: `4`
- Explanation: Replacing the middle `A` with `B` produces `"AABBBBA"`, whose `"BBBB"` substring has length `4`.
  Other replacement choices may attain the same maximum.
