## General
Given LeetCode wants to give one of its best employees the option to travel among `n` cities to collect algorithm problems. But all work and no play makes Jack a dull boy, you could take vacations in some particular cities ..., the algorithm executes a single-pass linear scan through input elements.

## Complexity detail
- **Time Complexity**: $O(kn^2)$ — Operation count bound.
- **Space Complexity**: $O(n)$ — Auxiliary memory allocation bound.
