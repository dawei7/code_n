## General
Given You have a water dispenser that can dispense cold, warm, and hot water. Every second, you can either fill up `2` cups with **different** types of water, or `1` cup of any type of water, the algorithm solves **Minimum Amount of Time to Fill Cups** directly.

## Complexity detail
- **Time Complexity**: $O(1)$ — Operation count bound.
- **Space Complexity**: $O(1)$ — Auxiliary memory allocation bound.
