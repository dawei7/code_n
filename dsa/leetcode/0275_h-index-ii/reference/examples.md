## Examples

**Example 1**

- Input: `citations = [0,1,3,5,6]`
- Output: `3`
- Explanation: The five entries report citation counts of `0`, `1`, `3`, `5`, and `6`. Three papers have at least three citations apiece, while the other two have no more than three, so the h-index is `3`.

**Example 2**

- Input: `citations = [1,2,100]`
- Output: `2`
