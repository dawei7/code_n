## General
Given There is a one-dimensional garden on the x-axis. The garden starts at the point `0` and ends at the point `n`. (i.e., the length of the garden is `n`), the algorithm executes a two-pointer approach to shrink boundaries or maintain a sliding window.

## Complexity detail
- **Time Complexity**: $O(n)$ — Operation count bound.
- **Space Complexity**: $O(n)$ — Auxiliary memory allocation bound.
