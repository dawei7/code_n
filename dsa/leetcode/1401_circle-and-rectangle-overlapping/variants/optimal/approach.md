## General
Given a circle represented as `(radius, xCenter, yCenter)` and an axis-aligned rectangle represented as `(x1, y1, x2, y2)`, where `(x1, y1)` are the coordinates of the bottom-left corner, and `(x2, y2)` are the coordinates ..., the algorithm solves **Circle and Rectangle Overlapping** directly.

## Complexity detail
- **Time Complexity**: $O(1)$ — Operation count bound.
- **Space Complexity**: $O(1)$ — Auxiliary memory allocation bound.
