## General
Given two positive integer arrays `spells` and `potions`, of length `n` and `m` respectively, where $\text{spells}[i]$ represents the strength of the $$i^{\text{th}}$$ spell and $\text{potions}[j]$ represents the strength o..., the algorithm executes a single-pass linear scan through input elements.

## Complexity detail
- **Time Complexity**: $O(m log m + n log m)$ — Operation count bound.
- **Space Complexity**: $O(m)$ — Auxiliary memory allocation bound.
