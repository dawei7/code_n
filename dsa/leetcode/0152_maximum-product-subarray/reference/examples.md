## Examples

**Example 1**

- Input: `nums = [2, 3, -2, 4]`
- Output: `6`
- Explanation: The subarray `[2, 3]` has product `6`, the largest available product.

**Example 2**

- Input: `nums = [-2, 0, -1]`
- Output: `0`
- Explanation: The answer cannot be `2`, because `[-2, -1]` skips the intervening `0` and therefore is not a subarray.
