## Constraints

- $m = \lvert \texttt{maze} \rvert$
- $n = \lvert \texttt{maze[i]} \rvert$
- $1 \le m, n \le 100$
- Every `maze[i][j]` is `0` or `1`.
- $\lvert \texttt{ball} \rvert = 2$
- $\lvert \texttt{hole} \rvert = 2$
- $0 \le \texttt{ballrow}, \texttt{holerow} \le m$
- $0 \le \texttt{ballcol}, \texttt{holecol} \le n$
- The ball and hole are both in empty spaces and begin at distinct positions.
- The maze contains at least two empty spaces.
