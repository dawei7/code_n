## Examples

**Example 1**

- Input: `n = 10203004`
- Output: `12340`
- Explanation:

  1. The nonzero digits, in their original order, are `1`, `2`, `3`, and `4`, so `x = 1234`.
  2. Their sum is `sum = 1 + 2 + 3 + 4 = 10`.
  3. Therefore, `x * sum = 1234 * 10 = 12340`.

**Example 2**

- Input: `n = 1000`
- Output: `1`
- Explanation:

  1. The only nonzero digit is `1`, giving `x = 1` and `sum = 1`.
  2. Therefore, `x * sum = 1 * 1 = 1`.
