## Examples

**Example 1**

- Input: `n = 20`
- Output: `17`
- Explanation: The prime values at most `20` that are also consecutive-prime sums beginning at `2` are:
  - `2 = 2`
  - `5 = 2 + 3`
  - `17 = 2 + 3 + 5 + 7`

  The greatest of them is `17`.

**Example 2**

- Input: `n = 2`
- Output: `2`
- Explanation: The only consecutive-prime sum that does not exceed `2` is the one-term sum `2` itself.
