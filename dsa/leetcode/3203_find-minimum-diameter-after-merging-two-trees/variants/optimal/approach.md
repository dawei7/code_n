## General
Given There exist two **undirected **trees with `n` and `m` nodes, numbered from `0` to $n - 1$ and from `0` to $m - 1$, respectively. You are given two 2D integer arrays `edges1` and `edges2` of lengths $n - 1$ and $m - 1$..., the algorithm executes depth-first search (DFS) recursion to explore valid decision branches. It utilizes a hash map (`dict`) to store element values and their indices/frequencies for $O(1)$ fast access.

## Complexity detail
- **Time Complexity**: $O(N)$ — Operation count bound.
- **Space Complexity**: $O(N)$ — Auxiliary memory allocation bound.
