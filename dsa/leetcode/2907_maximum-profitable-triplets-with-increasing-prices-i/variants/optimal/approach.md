## General
Given the **0-indexed** arrays `prices` and `profits` of length `n`. There are `n` items in an store where the $$i^{\text{th}}$$ item has a price of $\text{prices}[i]$ and a profit of $\text{profits}[i]$, the algorithm executes a two-pointer approach to shrink boundaries or maintain a sliding window.

## Complexity detail
- **Time Complexity**: $O(n^2)$ — Operation count bound.
- **Space Complexity**: $O(1)$ — Auxiliary memory allocation bound.
