## Examples

**Example 1**

- Input: `weight = [100,200,150,1000]`
- Output: `4`
- Explanation: All four apples fit because their combined weight is `1450`, which is within the basket's capacity.

**Example 2**

- Input: `weight = [900,950,800,1000,700,800]`
- Output: `5`
- Explanation: The combined weight of all six apples is greater than `5000`, so five apples are chosen; any five of these six fit.
