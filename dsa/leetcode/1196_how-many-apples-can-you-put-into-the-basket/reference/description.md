## Description

You have a collection of apples and one basket that can carry at most `5000` units of total weight. The integer array `weight` describes the apples individually: `weight[i]` is the weight of the $i$th apple.

Choose apples for the basket without letting their combined weight exceed its capacity. Return the maximum number of apples that can be carried at once. The requested result is only this greatest count, not a particular selection of apples.
