## Description

A garden has `height` rows and `width` columns. The tree stands at `tree = [tree_r, tree_c]`, the squirrel begins at `squirrel = [squirrel_r, squirrel_c]`, and each `nuts[i] = [nut_i_r, nut_i_c]` gives the position of one nut.

On each move, the squirrel may enter an adjacent cell by moving up, down, left, or right. It can carry at most one nut at a time. To collect a nut, it must reach that nut and then carry it to the tree before transporting another one.

Return the minimum number of moves needed to collect every nut and place the nuts under the tree one by one.
