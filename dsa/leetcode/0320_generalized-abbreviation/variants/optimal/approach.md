## General
Given A word's **generalized abbreviation** can be constructed by taking any number of **non-overlapping** and **non-adjacent** substrings and replacing them with their respective lengths, the algorithm executes depth-first search (DFS) recursion to explore valid decision branches.

## Complexity detail
- **Time Complexity**: $O(n 2^n)$ — Operation count bound.
- **Space Complexity**: $O(n 2^n)$ — Auxiliary memory allocation bound.
