## Description

A chocolate bar is arranged as a sequence of chunks, and `sweetness[i]` gives the sweetness of the $i$th chunk.

You want to share the bar with `k` friends. Make exactly `k` cuts to form `k + 1` pieces, with every piece containing one or more consecutive chunks from the original order.

You give the other pieces to your friends and keep the piece whose total sweetness is smallest. Choose the cut positions so that this minimum piece total is as large as possible, and return that maximum achievable sweetness.
