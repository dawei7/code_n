## Examples

**Example 1**

- Input: `d = [3,1], r = [2,3]`
- Output: `5`
- Explanation:

  - Drone 1 delivers at hours `1`, `3`, and `5`, while recharging at hours `2` and `4`.
  - Drone 2 delivers at hour `2`, while recharging at hour `3`.

**Example 2**

- Input: `d = [1,3], r = [2,2]`
- Output: `7`
- Explanation:

  - Drone 1 delivers at hour `3`, while recharging at hours `2`, `4`, and `6`.
  - Drone 2 delivers at hours `1`, `5`, and `7`, while recharging at hours `2`, `4`, and `6`.

**Example 3**

- Input: `d = [2,1], r = [3,4]`
- Output: `3`
- Explanation:

  - Drone 1 delivers at hours `1` and `2`, then recharges at hour `3`.
  - Drone 2 delivers at hour `3`.
