## General
Given Alice and Bob want to water `n` plants in their garden. The plants are arranged in a row and are labeled from `0` to $n - 1$ from left to right where the $$i^{\text{th}}$$ plant is located at $x = i$, the algorithm executes a two-pointer approach to shrink boundaries or maintain a sliding window.

## Complexity detail
- **Time Complexity**: $O(n)$ — Operation count bound.
- **Space Complexity**: $O(1)$ — Auxiliary memory allocation bound.
