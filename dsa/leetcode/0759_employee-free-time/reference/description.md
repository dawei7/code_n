## Description

Each employee's working time is recorded as a schedule of busy intervals. Within one employee's schedule, the intervals are sorted and do not overlap.

Find every time interval during which all employees are free. Report only finite intervals with positive length, ordered from earliest to latest. Consequently, free time extending without bound before the first busy interval or after the last busy interval is not part of the result, and two busy intervals that merely touch leave no reportable gap between them.
