## General
Given Bob is standing at cell `(0, 0)`, and he wants to reach `destination`: `(row, column)`. He can only travel **right** and **down**. You are going to help Bob by providing **instructions** for him to reach `destination`, the algorithm executes a single-pass linear scan through input elements.

## Complexity detail
- **Time Complexity**: $O(r+c)$ — Operation count bound.
- **Space Complexity**: $O(r+c)$ — Auxiliary memory allocation bound.
