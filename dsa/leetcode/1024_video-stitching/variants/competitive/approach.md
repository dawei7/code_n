## General
Given a series of video clips from a sporting event that lasted `time` seconds. These video clips can be overlapping with each other and have varying lengths, the algorithm executes a two-pointer approach to shrink boundaries or maintain a sliding window.

## Complexity detail
- **Time Complexity**: $O(N+T)$ — Operation count bound.
- **Space Complexity**: $O(T)$ — Auxiliary memory allocation bound.
