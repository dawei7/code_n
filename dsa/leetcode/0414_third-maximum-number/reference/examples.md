## Examples

**Example 1**

- Input: `nums = [3, 2, 1]`
- Output: `1`
- Explanation: The first, second, and third distinct maxima are $3$, $2$, and $1$, respectively.

**Example 2**

- Input: `nums = [1, 2]`
- Output: `2`
- Explanation: The distinct maxima are $2$ and $1$. A third one does not exist, so the overall maximum $2$ is
  returned.

**Example 3**

- Input: `nums = [2, 2, 3, 1]`
- Output: `1`
- Explanation: The distinct maxima are $3$, $2$, and $1$. Both occurrences of $2$ share one rank because their
  values are equal.
