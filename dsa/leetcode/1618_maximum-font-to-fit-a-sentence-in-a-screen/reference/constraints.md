## Constraints

- $1 \le \text{text.length} \le 10^5$.
- $1 \le w, h \le 10^5$.
- $1 \le \text{fonts.length} \le 10^5$.
- $1 \le \text{fonts}[i] \le 10^5$.
- `fonts` is sorted in strictly increasing order.
- `text` consists of lowercase English letters.
- `FontInfo.getWidth` and `FontInfo.getHeight` are non-decreasing with respect to font size.
