## General
Given A truck has two fuel tanks. You are given two integers, `mainTank` representing the fuel present in the main tank in liters and `additionalTank` representing the fuel present in the additional tank in liters, the algorithm solves **Total Distance Traveled** directly. Edge case handling: applies modulo arithmetic to prevent integer overflow.

## Complexity detail
- **Time Complexity**: $O(1)$ — Operation count bound.
- **Space Complexity**: $O(1)$ — Auxiliary memory allocation bound.
