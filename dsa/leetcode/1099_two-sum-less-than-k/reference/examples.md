## Examples

**Example 1**

- **Input:** `nums = [34,23,1,24,75,33,54,8], k = 60`
- **Output:** `58`
- **Explanation:** The values 34 and 24 form the sum 58, which is below 60.

**Example 2**

- **Input:** `nums = [10,20,30], k = 15`
- **Output:** `-1`
- **Explanation:** No pair has a sum strictly less than 15.
