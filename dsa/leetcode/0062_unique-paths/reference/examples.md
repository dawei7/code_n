## Examples

**Example 1**

- Input: `m = 3, n = 7`
- Output: `28`

The independently rendered grid marks the start and destination; all movement is right or down:

```text
S · · · · · ·
· · · · · · ·
· · · · · · T

allowed: → or ↓
```

**Example 2**

- Input: `m = 3, n = 2`
- Output: `3`
- Explanation: The three paths are `Right → Down → Down`, `Down → Down → Right`, and `Down → Right → Down`.
