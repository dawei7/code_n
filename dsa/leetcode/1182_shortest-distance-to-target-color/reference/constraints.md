## Constraints

- $1 \leq \lvert\texttt{colors}\rvert \leq 5 \cdot 10^4$
- `colors[i]` is `1`, `2`, or `3`.
- $1 \leq \lvert\texttt{queries}\rvert \leq 5 \cdot 10^4$
- Every `queries[i]` has exactly two entries.
- $0 \leq \texttt{queries[i][0]} < \lvert\texttt{colors}\rvert$
- `queries[i][1]` is `1`, `2`, or `3`.
