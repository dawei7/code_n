## General
Given two **positive** integers `startPos` and `endPos`. Initially, you are standing at position `startPos` on an **infinite** number line. Edge case handling: applies modulo arithmetic to prevent integer overflow.

## Complexity detail
- **Time Complexity**: $O(k)$ — Operation count bound.
