## General
Given two **0-indexed** integer arrays `nums` and `removeQueries`, both of length `n`. ## Complexity detail
- **Time Complexity**: $O(n alpha(n))$ — Operation count bound.
- **Space Complexity**: $O(n)$ — Auxiliary memory allocation bound.
