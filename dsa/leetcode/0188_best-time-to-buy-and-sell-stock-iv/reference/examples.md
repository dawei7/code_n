## Examples

**Example 1**

- Input: `k = 2, prices = [2, 4, 1]`
- Output: `2`
- Explanation: Buy on day `1` for `2` and sell on day `2` for `4`, earning `4 - 2 = 2`.

**Example 2**

- Input: `k = 2, prices = [3, 2, 6, 5, 0, 3]`
- Output: `7`
- Explanation: Buy on day `2` for `2` and sell on day `3` for `6`, earning `4`; then buy on day `5` for `0` and sell on day `6` for `3`, earning another `3`.
