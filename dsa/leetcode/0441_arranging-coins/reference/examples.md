## Examples

**Example 1**

- Input: `n = 5`
- Output: `2`
- Explanation: The third row is incomplete, so only two rows count.

| Staircase row | Coins placed | Required coins | Status |
|---:|---:|---:|---|
| 1 | 1 | 1 | Complete |
| 2 | 2 | 2 | Complete |
| 3 | 2 | 3 | Incomplete |

**Example 2**

- Input: `n = 8`
- Output: `3`
- Explanation: The fourth row is incomplete, so three rows count.

| Staircase row | Coins placed | Required coins | Status |
|---:|---:|---:|---|
| 1 | 1 | 1 | Complete |
| 2 | 2 | 2 | Complete |
| 3 | 3 | 3 | Complete |
| 4 | 2 | 4 | Incomplete |
