## Constraints

- $1 \le \texttt{width},\texttt{height} \le 10^4$
- $1 \le \texttt{food.length} \le 50$
- `food[i].length == 2`
- $0 \le r_i < \texttt{height}$
- $0 \le c_i < \texttt{width}$
- `direction.length == 1`
- `direction` is `"U"`, `"D"`, `"L"`, or `"R"`.
- At most $10^4$ calls are made to `move`.
