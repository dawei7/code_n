## General
Given There is a biker going on a road trip. The road trip consists of $n + 1$ points at various altitudes. The biker starts his trip on point `0` with altitude equal `0`, the algorithm solves **Find the Highest Altitude** directly.

## Complexity detail
- **Time Complexity**: $O(n)$ — Operation count bound.
- **Space Complexity**: $O(1)$ — Auxiliary memory allocation bound.
