## General
Given A shop is selling candies at a discount. For **every two** candies sold, the shop gives a **third** candy for **free**, the algorithm solves **Minimum Cost of Buying Candies With Discount** directly.

## Complexity detail
- **Time Complexity**: $O(n log n)$ — Operation count bound.
- **Space Complexity**: $O(n)$ — Auxiliary memory allocation bound.
