## Constraints

- The number of nodes in the tree is in the range $[1, 10^5]$.
- `Node.val` is one of `0`, `1`, `2`, `3`, `4`, or `5`.
- Leaf nodes (`val` is `0` or `1`) have no children.
- Operator nodes `2` (OR), `3` (AND), and `4` (XOR) have exactly 2 children.
- Operator node `5` (NOT) has exactly 1 child.
