## Examples

**Example 1**

- Input: `root = [10,5,15,1,8,null,7]`
- Output: `3`
- Explanation: The largest BST is the subtree rooted at `5`, containing nodes `5`, `1`, and `8`; therefore its size is `3`.

```text
          10
         /  \
       [5]   15
       / \     \
     [1] [8]    7
```

**Example 2**

- Input: `root = [4,2,7,2,3,5,null,2,null,null,null,null,null,1]`
- Output: `2`
