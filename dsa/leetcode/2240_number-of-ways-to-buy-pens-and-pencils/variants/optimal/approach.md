## General
Given an integer `total` indicating the amount of money you have. You are also given two integers `cost1` and `cost2` indicating the price of a pen and pencil respectively. You can spend **part or all** of your money to buy..., the algorithm executes a single-pass linear scan through input elements.

## Complexity detail
- **Time Complexity**: $O(T / max(c1, c2))$ — Operation count bound.
- **Space Complexity**: $O(1)$ — Auxiliary memory allocation bound.
