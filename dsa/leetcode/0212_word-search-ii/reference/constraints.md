## Constraints

- $m = \texttt{board.length}$
- $n = \texttt{board[i].length}$
- $1 \le m,n \le 12$
- Each `board[i][j]` is a lowercase English letter.
- $1 \le \texttt{words.length} \le 3 \times 10^4$
- $1 \le \texttt{words[i].length} \le 10$
- Every `words[i]` consists of lowercase English letters.
- All strings in `words` are unique.
