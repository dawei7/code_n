## Constraints

- $1 \le \lvert \texttt{key} \rvert \le 10$
- Each `key` contains only lowercase English letters.
- Before every `dec(key)` call, `key` is guaranteed to exist in the structure.
- Across one execution, at most $5 \times 10^4$ calls are made to `inc`, `dec`, `getMaxKey`, and `getMinKey`.
