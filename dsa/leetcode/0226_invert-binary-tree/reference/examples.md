## Examples

**Example 1**

```text
before                 after
       4                  4
     /   \              /   \
    2     7            7     2
   / \   / \          / \   / \
  1   3 6   9        9   6 3   1
```

- Input: `root = [4,2,7,1,3,6,9]`
- Output: `[4,7,2,9,6,3,1]`

**Example 2**

```text
before       after
    2           2
   / \         / \
  1   3       3   1
```

- Input: `root = [2,1,3]`
- Output: `[2,3,1]`

**Example 3**

- Input: `root = []`
- Output: `[]`
