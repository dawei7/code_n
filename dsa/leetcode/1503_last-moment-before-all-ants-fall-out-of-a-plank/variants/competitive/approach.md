## General
Given We have a wooden plank of the length `n` **units**. Some ants are walking on the plank, each ant moves with a speed of **1 unit per second**. Some of the ants move to the **left**, the other move to the **right**, the algorithm solves **Last Moment Before All Ants Fall Out of a Plank** directly.

## Complexity detail
- **Time Complexity**: $O(A)$ — Operation count bound.
- **Space Complexity**: $O(1)$ — Auxiliary memory allocation bound.
