## General
Given four integers, `m`, `n`, `introvertsCount`, and `extrovertsCount`. You have an `m x n` grid, and there are two types of people: introverts and extroverts. There are `introvertsCount` introverts and `extrovertsCount` e..., the algorithm executes depth-first search (DFS) recursion to explore valid decision branches.

## Complexity detail
- **Time Complexity**: $O(rc3^c(I+1)(E+1))$ — Operation count bound.
- **Space Complexity**: $O(rc3^c(I+1)(E+1))$ — Auxiliary memory allocation bound.
