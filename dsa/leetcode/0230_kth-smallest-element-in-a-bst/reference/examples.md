## Examples

**Example 1**

```text
      3
     / \
    1   4
     \
      2       ascending values: 1,2,3,4
```

- Input: `root = [3,1,4,null,2], k = 1`
- Output: `1`

**Example 2**

```text
        5
       / \
      3   6
     / \
    2   4
   /
  1             ascending values: 1,2,3,4,5,6
```

- Input: `root = [5,3,6,2,4,null,null,1], k = 3`
- Output: `3`
