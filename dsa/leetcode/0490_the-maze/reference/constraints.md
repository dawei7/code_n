## Constraints

- $m = \lvert \texttt{maze} \rvert$
- $n = \lvert \texttt{maze[i]} \rvert$
- $1 \le m, n \le 100$
- Every `maze[i][j]` is `0` or `1`.
- $\lvert \texttt{start} \rvert = 2$
- $\lvert \texttt{destination} \rvert = 2$
- $0 \le \texttt{startrow}, \texttt{destinationrow} < m$
- $0 \le \texttt{startcol}, \texttt{destinationcol} < n$
- The ball and destination are both in empty spaces and begin at distinct positions.
- The maze contains at least two empty spaces.
