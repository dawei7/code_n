## General
Given an integer array `arr`. From some starting index, you can make a series of jumps. The (1^st, 3^rd, 5^th, ...) jumps in the series are called **odd-numbered jumps**, and the (2^nd, 4^th, 6^th, ...) jumps in the series ..., the algorithm executes a single-pass linear scan through input elements.

## Complexity detail
- **Time Complexity**: $O(N\log N)$ — Operation count bound.
- **Space Complexity**: $O(N)$ — Auxiliary memory allocation bound.
