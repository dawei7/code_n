| ---: | ---: | --- | --- | --- | ---: |
| 0 | 1 | `[2, 3]` | `[1, 1]` | `[2, 3]` | 2 |
| 1 | 2 | `[3]` | `[1]` | `[]` | 0 |
| 2 | 1 | `[]` | `[]` | `[]` | 0 |
| 3 | 1 | `[]` | `[]` | `[]` | 0 |

Thus, `ans = [2, 0, 0, 0]`.
