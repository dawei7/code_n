## Examples

**Example 1**

- Input: `triangle = [[2], [3, 4], [6, 5, 7], [4, 1, 8, 3]]`
- Output: `11`
- Explanation: The triangle and its minimum path are shown below. The selected values sum to `2 + 3 + 5 + 1 = 11`.

```text
       [2]
      [3]  4
     6  [5]  7
    4  [1]  8   3
```

**Example 2**

- Input: `triangle = [[-10]]`
- Output: `-10`
