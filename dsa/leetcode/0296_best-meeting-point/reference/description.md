## Description

In an $m \times n$ binary grid `grid`, every cell containing `1` is the home of one friend. Choose a meeting point that minimizes the friends' combined travel distance and return that minimum total.

The total travel distance is the sum of every friend's distance from home to the meeting point. Distance is measured with the Manhattan metric:

$$
\operatorname{distance}(p_1,p_2)=\lvert p_2.x-p_1.x\rvert+\lvert p_2.y-p_1.y\rvert.
$$
