## Examples

**Example 1**

- Input: `nums = [5,2,2,4], k = 2`
- Output: `5`
- Explanation:
  - The two largest elements are `4` and `5`, whose sum is `4 + 5 = 9`.
  - The two smallest elements are `2` and `2`, whose sum is `2 + 2 = 4`.
  - Their absolute difference is `abs(9 - 4) = 5`.

**Example 2**

- Input: `nums = [100], k = 1`
- Output: `0`
- Explanation:
  - The largest element is `100`.
  - The smallest element is also `100`.
  - Their absolute difference is `abs(100 - 100) = 0`.
