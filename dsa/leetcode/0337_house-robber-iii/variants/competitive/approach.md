## General
Given The thief has found himself a new place for his thievery again. There is only one entrance to this area, called `root`, the algorithm solves **House Robber III** directly. It utilizes binary tree node references (`val`, `left`, `right`) to traverse structural hierarchies. Edge case handling: guards against empty/null inputs via early returns.
