## General
Given two strings `word1` and `word2`. You want to construct a string `merge` in the following way: while either `word1` or `word2` are non-empty, choose **one** of the following options:, the algorithm solves **Largest Merge Of Two Strings** directly.

## Complexity detail
- **Time Complexity**: $O(S^2)$ — Operation count bound.
- **Space Complexity**: $O(S)$ — Auxiliary memory allocation bound.
