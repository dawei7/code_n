## General
Given Koko loves to eat bananas. There are `n` piles of bananas, the $$i^{\text{th}}$$ pile has $\text{piles}[i]$ bananas. The guards have gone and will come back in `h` hours, the algorithm executes a single-pass linear scan through input elements.

## Complexity detail
- **Time Complexity**: $O(n\log M)$ — Operation count bound.
- **Space Complexity**: $O(1)$ — Auxiliary memory allocation bound.
