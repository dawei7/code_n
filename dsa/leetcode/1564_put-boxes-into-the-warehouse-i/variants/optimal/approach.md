## General
Given two arrays of positive integers, `boxes` and `warehouse`, representing the heights of some boxes of unit width and the heights of `n` rooms in a warehouse respectively. ## Complexity detail
- **Time Complexity**: $O(B \log B + W)$ — Operation count bound.
- **Space Complexity**: $O(B + W)$ — Auxiliary memory allocation bound.
