## General
Given You have `n` flower seeds. Every seed must be planted first before it can begin to grow, then bloom. Planting a seed takes time and so does the growth of a seed. You are given two **0-indexed** integer arrays `plantTi..., the algorithm executes a single-pass linear scan through input elements.

## Complexity detail
- **Time Complexity**: $O(n log n)$ — Operation count bound.
