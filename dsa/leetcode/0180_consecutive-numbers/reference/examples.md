## Examples

**Example 1**

- Input: `Logs = [[1,1],[2,1],[3,1],[4,2],[5,1],[6,2],[7,2]]`

| id | num |
|---:|---:|
| 1 | 1 |
| 2 | 1 |
| 3 | 1 |
| 4 | 2 |
| 5 | 1 |
| 6 | 2 |
| 7 | 2 |

- Output: `ConsecutiveNums = [1]`

| ConsecutiveNums |
|---:|
| 1 |

- Explanation: `1` is the only value that occurs at least three times in uninterrupted succession.
