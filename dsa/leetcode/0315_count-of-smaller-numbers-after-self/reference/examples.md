## Examples

**Example 1**

- Input: `nums = [5,2,6,1]`
- Output: `[2,1,1,0]`
- Explanation: To the right of `5`, the two smaller values are `2` and `1`. Only `1` is smaller than `2` on its right; only `1` is smaller than `6` on its right; and no element follows the final `1`.

**Example 2**

- Input: `nums = [-1]`
- Output: `[0]`

**Example 3**

- Input: `nums = [-1,-1]`
- Output: `[0,0]`
