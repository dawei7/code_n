## General
Given Consider all the leaves of a binary tree, from left to right order, the values of those leaves form a **leaf value sequence***.*, the algorithm executes a two-pointer approach to shrink boundaries or maintain a sliding window. ## Complexity detail
- **Time Complexity**: $O(n_1+n_2)$ — Operation count bound.
