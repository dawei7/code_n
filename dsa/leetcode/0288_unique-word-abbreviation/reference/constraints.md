## Constraints

- $1 \le \texttt{dictionary.length} \le 3 \cdot 10^4$
- $1 \le \texttt{dictionary[i].length} \le 20$
- Every `dictionary[i]` contains only lowercase English letters.
- $1 \le \texttt{word.length} \le 20$
- Every query `word` contains only lowercase English letters.
- At most $5000$ calls are made to `isUnique`.
