## Constraints

- $1 \le \texttt{s.length} \le 3 \times 10^5$
- `s` consists of integers and the operators `+`, `-`, `*`, and `/`, separated by any number of spaces.
- `s` is a valid expression.
- Every integer in the expression is non-negative and at most $2^{31}-1$.
- The final answer is guaranteed to fit in a signed 32-bit integer.
