## Constraints

- $1 \le \lvert\texttt{nums}\rvert \le 10^5$.
- $1 \le \texttt{nums[i]} \le 10^9$.
- `nums` is strictly increasing.
- $1 \le \lvert\texttt{queries}\rvert \le 10^5$.
- Every query has the form `[l_i, r_i, k_i]`.
- $0 \le l_i \le r_i < \lvert\texttt{nums}\rvert$.
- $1 \le k_i \le 10^9$.
