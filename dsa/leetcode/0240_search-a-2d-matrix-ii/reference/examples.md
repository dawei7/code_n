## Examples

**Example 1**

```text
 1   4   7  11  15
 2  [5]  8  12  19    target 5 is present
 3   6   9  16  22
10  13  14  17  24
18  21  23  26  30
```

- Input: `matrix = [[1,4,7,11,15],[2,5,8,12,19],[3,6,9,16,22],[10,13,14,17,24],[18,21,23,26,30]], target = 5`
- Output: `true`
