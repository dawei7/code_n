## Examples

**Example 1**

- Input: `n = 4`
- Output: `false`
- Explanation: Removing one stone lets the friend take the remaining three; removing two lets the friend take two; and removing three lets the friend take the final stone. Every possible first move therefore loses against an optimal response.

**Example 2**

- Input: `n = 1`
- Output: `true`

**Example 3**

- Input: `n = 2`
- Output: `true`
