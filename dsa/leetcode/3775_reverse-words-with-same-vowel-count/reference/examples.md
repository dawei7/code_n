## Examples

**Example 1**

- Input: `s = "cat and mice"`
- Output: `"cat dna mice"`
- Explanation:
  - The first word `"cat"` has one vowel.
  - `"and"` also has one vowel, so it becomes `"dna"`.
  - `"mice"` has two vowels and remains unchanged.
  - The final string is `"cat dna mice"`.

**Example 2**

- Input: `s = "book is nice"`
- Output: `"book is ecin"`
- Explanation:
  - The first word `"book"` has two vowels.
  - `"is"` has one vowel and remains unchanged.
  - `"nice"` has two vowels, so it becomes `"ecin"`.
  - The final string is `"book is ecin"`.

**Example 3**

- Input: `s = "banana healthy"`
- Output: `"banana healthy"`
- Explanation:
  - The first word `"banana"` has three vowels.
  - `"healthy"` has two vowels and therefore remains unchanged.
  - The final string is `"banana healthy"`.
