## Examples

**Example 1**

- Input: `prices = [7, 1, 5, 3, 6, 4]`
- Output: `7`
- Explanation: Buy on day 2 for `1` and sell on day 3 for `5`, earning `4`. Then buy on day 4 for `3` and sell on day 5 for `6`, earning `3`. The total is `4 + 3 = 7`.

**Example 2**

- Input: `prices = [1, 2, 3, 4, 5]`
- Output: `4`
- Explanation: Buying on day 1 for `1` and selling on day 5 for `5` earns `5 - 1 = 4`, so the total profit is `4`.

**Example 3**

- Input: `prices = [7, 6, 4, 3, 1]`
- Output: `0`
- Explanation: No positive-profit trade exists, so never buying produces the maximum profit of `0`.
