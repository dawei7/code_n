## Examples

**Example 1**

- **Input:** `root = [1,2,3,null,4,5,6]`, `u = 4`
- **Output:** `5`
- **Explanation:** At depth 2, nodes are 4, 5, 6 from left to right. The node to the right of 4 is 5.

**Example 2**

- **Input:** `root = [3,null,4,2]`, `u = 2`
- **Output:** `null`
- **Explanation:** Node 2 is the rightmost node on its level.

**Example 3**

- **Input:** `root = [1]`, `u = 1`
- **Output:** `null`
- **Explanation:** Root node 1 is the only node on its level.
