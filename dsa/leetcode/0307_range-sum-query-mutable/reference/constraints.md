## Constraints

- $1 \le \texttt{nums.length} \le 3 \cdot 10^4$
- $-100 \le \texttt{nums[i]} \le 100$
- $0 \le \texttt{index} < \texttt{nums.length}$
- $-100 \le \texttt{val} \le 100$
- $0 \le \texttt{left} \le \texttt{right} < \texttt{nums.length}$
- At most $3 \cdot 10^4$ total calls are made to `update` and `sumRange`.
