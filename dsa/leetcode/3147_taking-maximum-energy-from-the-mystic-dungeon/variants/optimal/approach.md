## General
Given In a mystic dungeon, `n` magicians are standing in a line. Each magician has an attribute that gives you energy. Some magicians can give you negative energy, which means taking energy from you, the algorithm executes a single-pass linear scan through input elements.

## Complexity detail
- **Time Complexity**: $O(n)$ — Operation count bound.
- **Space Complexity**: $O(k)$ — Auxiliary memory allocation bound.
