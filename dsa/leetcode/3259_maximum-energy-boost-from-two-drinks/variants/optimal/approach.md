## General
Given two integer arrays `energyDrinkA` and `energyDrinkB` of the same length `n` by a futuristic sports scientist. These arrays represent the energy boosts per hour provided by two different energy drinks, A and B, respect..., the algorithm executes a single-pass linear scan through input elements.

## Complexity detail
- **Time Complexity**: $O(n)$ — Operation count bound.
- **Space Complexity**: $O(1)$ — Auxiliary memory allocation bound.
