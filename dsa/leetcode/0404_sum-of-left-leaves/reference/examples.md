## Examples

**Example 1**

- Input: `root = [3,9,20,null,null,15,7]`
- Output: `24`
- Explanation: Nodes `9` and `15` are the two left leaves, and their values sum to `24`.

The source tree illustration is represented below; brackets mark the contributing leaves.

```text
        3
       / \
    [9]   20
          / \
       [15]  7
```

**Example 2**

- Input: `root = [1]`
- Output: `0`
