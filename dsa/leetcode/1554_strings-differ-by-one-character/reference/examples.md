## Examples

**Example 1**

- **Input:** `dict = ["abcd", "acbd", "aacd"]`
- **Output:** `true`
- **Explanation:** `"abcd"` and `"aacd"` differ only at their second character (index 1).

**Example 2**

- **Input:** `dict = ["ab", "cd", "yz"]`
- **Output:** `false`
- **Explanation:** Every pair of strings differs at 2 positions.

**Example 3**

- **Input:** `dict = ["abcd", "cccc", "abyd", "abab"]`
- **Output:** `true`
- **Explanation:** `"abcd"` and `"abyd"` differ only at index 2.
