## General
Given There are `n` gas stations along a circular route, where the amount of gas at the $$i^{\text{th}}$$ station is $\text{gas}[i]$, the algorithm executes a single-pass linear scan through input elements.

## Complexity detail
- **Time Complexity**: $O(n)$ — Operation count bound.
- **Space Complexity**: $O(1)$ — Auxiliary memory allocation bound.
