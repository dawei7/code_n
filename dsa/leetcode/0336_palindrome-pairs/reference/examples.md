## Examples

**Example 1**

- Input: `words = ["abcd","dcba","lls","s","sssll"]`
- Output: `[[0,1],[1,0],[3,2],[2,4]]`
- Explanation: The four concatenated palindromes are `"abcddcba"`, `"dcbaabcd"`, `"slls"`, and `"llssssll"`.

**Example 2**

- Input: `words = ["bat","tab","cat"]`
- Output: `[[0,1],[1,0]]`
- Explanation: The resulting palindromes are `"battab"` and `"tabbat"`.

**Example 3**

- Input: `words = ["a",""]`
- Output: `[[0,1],[1,0]]`
- Explanation: Either order concatenates to the palindrome `"a"`.
