## Constraints

- $1 \le n = \lvert\texttt{labels}\rvert \le 5 \cdot 10^4$
- $0 \le \lvert\texttt{edges}\rvert \le 5 \cdot 10^4$
- `edges[i].length == 3`
- $0 \le \texttt{u_i}, \texttt{v_i} < n$
- $\texttt{u_i} \ne \texttt{v_i}$
- $1 \le \texttt{w_i} \le 10^4$
- `labels` consists only of lowercase English letters.
- $1 \le k \le 50$
