## Constraints

- `1 <= beginWord.length <= 5`
- `endWord.length == beginWord.length`
- `1 <= wordList.length <= 500`
- `wordList[i].length == beginWord.length`
- `beginWord`, `endWord`, and every `wordList[i]` contain only lowercase English letters.
- `beginWord != endWord`
- All words in `wordList` are unique.
- The sum of the lengths of all shortest transformation sequences is at most $10^5$.
