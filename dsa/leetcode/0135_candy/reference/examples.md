## Examples

**Example 1**

- Input: `ratings = [1, 0, 2]`
- Output: `5`
- Explanation: Giving the three children `2`, `1`, and `2` candies, respectively, satisfies the conditions with a total of `5`.

**Example 2**

- Input: `ratings = [1, 2, 2]`
- Output: `4`
- Explanation: A valid minimum assignment is `1`, `2`, and `1` candies. The third child may receive one candy because its rating equals, rather than exceeds, the second child's rating.
