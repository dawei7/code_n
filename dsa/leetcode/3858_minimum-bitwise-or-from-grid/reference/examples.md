## Examples

**Example 1**

- Input: `grid = [[1,5],[2,4]]`
- Output: `3`
- Explanation:
  - Choose `1` from the first row and `2` from the second row.
  - Their bitwise OR is `1 | 2 = 3`, which is the minimum possible.

**Example 2**

- Input: `grid = [[3,5],[6,4]]`
- Output: `5`
- Explanation:
  - Choose `5` from the first row and `4` from the second row.
  - Their bitwise OR is `5 | 4 = 5`, the smallest attainable result.

**Example 3**

- Input: `grid = [[7,9,8]]`
- Output: `7`
- Explanation: With only one row, choosing `7` gives the minimum bitwise OR.
