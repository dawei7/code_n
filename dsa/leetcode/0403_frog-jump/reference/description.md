## Description

A frog must cross a river by landing only on stones positioned at integer units. The stone positions are supplied in ascending order. The frog begins on the first stone, and its first jump must be exactly one unit.

After a jump of length `k`, the next jump must have length `k - 1`, `k`, or `k + 1`. Every jump moves forward.

Return whether some legal sequence of landings reaches the final stone.
