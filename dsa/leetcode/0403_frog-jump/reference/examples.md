## Examples

**Example 1**

- Input: `stones = [0,1,3,5,6,8,12,17]`
- Output: `true`
- Explanation: One successful sequence uses jump lengths `1,2,2,3,4,5`, landing on the second, third, fourth, sixth, seventh, and eighth stones.

**Example 2**

- Input: `stones = [0,1,2,3,4,8,9,11]`
- Output: `false`
- Explanation: The gap between the fifth and sixth stones is too large to cross under the permitted jump-length changes.
