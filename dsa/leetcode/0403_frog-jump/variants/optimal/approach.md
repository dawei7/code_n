## General
Given A frog is crossing a river. The river is divided into some number of units, and at each unit, there may or may not exist a stone. The frog can jump on a stone, but it must not jump into the water, the algorithm executes depth-first search (DFS) recursion to explore valid decision branches.

## Complexity detail
- **Time Complexity**: $O(n^2)$ — Operation count bound.
- **Space Complexity**: $O(n^2)$ — Auxiliary memory allocation bound.
