## Description

For a positive integer $n$, let $\sigma_2(n)$ be the sum of the squares of its divisors. For example,

$$
\sigma_2(10) = 1 + 4 + 25 + 100 = 130.
$$

Find the sum of all $n$, $0 \lt n \lt 64\,000\,000$ such that $\sigma_2(n)$ is a perfect square.


## Function Contract

- `solve() -> int`
