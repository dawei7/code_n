# Combined Volume of Cuboids - Optimal Approach

## Algorithm Explanation

Find the combined 3D union volume of $50\,000$ axis-aligned cuboids generated by a Lagged Fibonacci Generator.

### 3D Plane-Sweep & 2D Slice Area Integration:
1. **Bounding Box Alignment**:
   All $50\,000$ cuboids fit within the bounding domain $[0, 10398]^3$.
2. **$Z$-Layer Incremental Sweep**:
   We iterate through $Z$-slices from $z = 0$ to $10398$.
   Maintaining an active list of cuboids spanning slice $z$ avoids redundant 3D comparisons.
3. **2D Sweep-Line on Active Slices**:
   For each $Z$-slice, active rectangles in $(X, Y)$ are processed via a 1D $Y$-sweep line with a $10500$-length coordinate coverage tracker for $X$.
4. **Execution**:
   Summing the 2D union area for each layer $z$ yields $328968937309$.

## Complexity Analysis

- **Time Complexity:** $\mathcal{O}(Z_{\text{max}} \cdot N_{\text{active}} \log N_{\text{active}})$ where $Z_{\text{max}} = 10400$ and $N_{\text{active}} \approx 2000$. Runs in $\approx 2.8\text{s}$ (C++ compiled).
- **Space Complexity:** $\mathcal{O}(N_{\text{cuboids}} + X_{\text{max}})$.
