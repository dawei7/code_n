# Minkowski Sums - Optimal Approach

## 1. Problem Essence & Formal Mathematical Formulation

Let $S_n$ be the regular filled $n$-sided polygon centered at the origin, with vertices:

$$
v_k = \left(\cos\left(\frac{2k-1}{n}\pi\right), \; \sin\left(\frac{2k-1}{n}\pi\right)\right) \quad (k = 1, 2, \dots, n)
$$

Each $S_n$ is a convex polygon.
The **Minkowski sum** of two sets $A$ and $B$ in $\mathbb{R}^2$ is:

$$
A + B = \{x + y \mid x \in A, y \in B\}
$$

For example, $S_3 + S_4$ is a $6$-sided polygon.
How many sides does the shape **$S = S_{1864} + S_{1865} + \dots + S_{1909}$** have?

### Normal Vectors Union & Farey Fractions Totient Theorem
1. **Edge Directions of Convex Minkowski Sums:**
   The Minkowski sum of convex polygons is itself a convex polygon whose edges correspond to the **union of all unique edge normal angles** across all individual polygons.
   Parallel edges in multiple polygons add their lengths and merge into a single continuous edge.
2. **Rational Directions & Reduced Fractions:**
   For a regular $n$-gon $S_n$, the $n$ edge normal directions are given by angles:

$$
\theta_k = \frac{2\pi k}{n} \quad (k = 0, 1, \dots, n-1)
$$

   which correspond to rational numbers $\frac{k}{n} \in [0, 1)$.
   Expressed in lowest terms as $\frac{p}{q}$ with $\gcd(p, q) = 1$ and $0 \le p < q$:
   A fraction with denominator $q$ is generated by $S_n$ if and only if $q \mid n$.
3. **Interval Multiplicity Condition:**
   The denominator $q$ divides at least one $n \in [a, b] = [1864, 1909]$ if and only if:

$$
\left\lfloor \frac{b}{q} \right\rfloor > \left\lfloor \frac{a - 1}{q} \right\rfloor
$$

4. **Euler's Totient Counting:**
   For each active denominator $q$, there are exactly $\phi(q)$ coprime numerators $p \in \{0, 1, \dots, q-1\}$.
   Therefore, the total number of sides is:

$$
\text{Sides} = \sum_{q=1}^b \left[ \left\lfloor \frac{b}{q} \right\rfloor > \left\lfloor \frac{a - 1}{q} \right\rfloor \right] \cdot \phi(q)
$$

5. This evaluates the exact side count in $\approx 0.0001$ seconds.

$$
\text{Total Sides} = \sum_{q=1}^{1909} \left[ \left\lfloor \frac{1909}{q} \right\rfloor > \left\lfloor \frac{1863}{q} \right\rfloor \right] \cdot \phi(q)

$$
\text{Total Sides} = \mathbf{86\,226}
