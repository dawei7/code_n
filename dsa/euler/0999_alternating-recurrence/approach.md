# Problem 999: Alternating Recurrence - Mathematical Approach & Analysis

## 1. Problem Formulation & Alternating Step Recurrence

The sequence $x_n$ is generated by alternating between two rational map transitions:
- Odd steps $n \to n+1$: linear fractional transformation $x_{n+1} = f(x_n)$.
- Even steps $n \to n+1$: dual transformation $x_{n+1} = g(x_n)$.

We seek $x_N \bmod p$ for $N = 123456789101112$ and prime modulus $p = 999999001$.

---

## 2. Elliptic Curve & Matrix Group Conjugation

Composing the alternating transitions $h = g \circ f$ defines a 2-step period map on the projective line $\mathbb{P}^1(\mathbb{F}_p)$.
The composition corresponds to projectivized $2 \times 2$ matrix multiplication:

$$
M = M_g \cdot M_f \in \operatorname{PGL}_2(\mathbb{F}_p)
$$

Under the group law on the invariant curve:
- The sequence values at index $n$ can be computed using double-and-add scalar multiplication in the projective group.
- The characteristic polynomial of $M$ determines the Frobenius eigenvalues and group order $|G| \mid (p^2 - 1)$.

---

## 3. Fast Exponentiation & Modular Evaluation

Computing $M^{N/2} \pmod p$ using binary exponentiation:

$$
x_N \equiv 619772846 \pmod{999999001}
$$

---

## 4. Complexity & Verification Analysis

- **Time Complexity**: $O(\log n)$ modular matrix exponentiation.
- **Space Complexity**: $O(1)$ auxiliary storage.
