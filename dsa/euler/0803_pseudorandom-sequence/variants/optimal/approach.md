## 3. Core Intuition & Mathematical Structure

### Bit-Sliced Chinese Remainder Search & 2-Adic Discrete Logarithm
1. **Truncated LCG State Recovery (Pattern Matching)**:
   A character $b_n = \lfloor a_n / 2^{16} \rfloor \bmod 52$ gives constraints modulo 4 and modulo 13.
   By splitting the 48-bit state into $a = x + 2^{24} y$ with $x = u + 2^{18} w$:
   - The low 18 bits $u_0$ are constrained modulo 4 across the string window (search space $2^{18}$).
   - The middle 6 bits $w_0$ (search space 64) determine the carries and residue system modulo 13.
   - The high 24 bits $y_0$ are reconstructed via affine arithmetic modulo $2^{24}$ and 13.
2. **2-Adic Discrete Logarithm for LCG Indexing**:
   Given any target 48-bit state $a_n$ and initial state $a_0$:
   $$a_n - a_0 = (a_1 - a_0) \sum_{k=0}^{n-1} A^k = (a_1 - a_0) \frac{A^n - 1}{A - 1} \pmod{2^{48}}$$
   Since $a_1 - a_0$ is odd, it is invertible in $\mathbb{Z}/2^{48}\mathbb{Z}$.
   We solve $A^n \equiv H \pmod{2^{48}}$ using bit-by-bit 2-adic lifting in $O(\log^2 2^{48}) = O(48)$ steps!
