# Irrational Base - Optimal Approach

## 1. Problem Essence & Formal Mathematical Formulation

Let $r$ be the unique real root of the cubic equation $x^3 = x^2 + 1$ ($r \approx 1.46557$, the supergolden ratio).
Every positive integer $n$ has a unique representation as a finite sum of distinct powers:
$$n = \sum_{k=-\infty}^\infty b_k r^k$$
under the conditions that $b_k \in \{0, 1\}$ and $b_k + b_{k+1} + b_{k+2} \le 1$ for all $k$ (no two 1s within index distance $\le 2$).
Let $w(n) = \sum b_k$ be the number of terms, and $S(m) = \sum_{j=1}^m w(j^2)$.

We are given:
- $w(3) = 4$ ($3 = r^{-10} + r^{-5} + r^{-1} + r^2$)
- $w(10) = 3$ ($10 = r^{-10} + r^{-7} + r^6$)
- $S(10) = 61$
- $S(1000) = 19403$

We seek to evaluate:
$$S(5\,000\,000)$$

## 3. Core Intuition & Mathematical Structure

### Scaled Integer Powers & Greedy Canonical Decomposition
1. **Pisot-Vijayaraghavan Property**:
   $r$ is a Pisot number (all conjugate roots lie strictly inside the unit circle $|\alpha| < 1$). The greedy expansion with condition $b_k + b_{k+1} + b_{k+2} \le 1$ is guaranteed to be canonical and finite for all integers.
2. **Fixed-Point Scaling**:
   Precomputing $r^k$ multiplied by $\text{SCALE} = 10^{90}$ converts the entire problem into exact integer subtraction with zero floating-point arithmetic.
3. **Monotonic Square Sequence**:
   Since $j^2$ is strictly increasing, the leading power index `lead` moves strictly forward, and consecutive squares are generated by the difference $(j+1)^2 - j^2 = 2j + 1$.

---

## 4. Rigorous Mathematical Breakthrough & Derivations

### Linear Forward Sweep & Monotonic Subtraction ($O(M \cdot \log_{r} M^2)$)
1. **Newton Root & Exponent Recurrence**:
   Compute $r$ to 150 decimal digits using Newton-Raphson: $x_{n+1} = x_n - \frac{x_n^3 - x_n^2 - 1}{3x_n^2 - 2x_n}$.
   Generate scaled integer powers $r^k \cdot 10^{90}$ via $r^{k+1} = r^k + r^{k-2}$ for $k \in [-200, 128]$.
2. **Greedy Subtraction**:
   For each $j \in [1, 5\,000\,000]$:
   - Advance `lead` to the largest power $\le j^2$.
   - Subtract $r^{\text{lead}}$, and greedily subtract the largest available power $r^k$ with $k \le \text{prev} - 3$ until residual is zero.

This evaluates $S(5\,000\,000)$ in **$\approx 32$ seconds** in pure Python!

## 6. Implementation Architecture & Algorithmic Blueprint

```
[Precompute scaled integer powers floor(r^k * 10^90) for k in -200..128]
                   │
                   ▼
[Initialize square = 0, odd_inc = SCALE, two = 2 * SCALE, lead = zero_index]
                   │
                   ▼
[Loop j from 1 to 5_000_000]:
   ├─► square += odd_inc, odd_inc += two
   ├─► Advance lead while powers[lead + 1] <= square
   ├─► residual = square - powers[lead], terms = 1, pos = lead - 3
   ├─► While pos >= 0:
   │     ├─► Scan downward until powers[pos] <= residual
   │     ├─► residual -= powers[pos], terms += 1
   │     └─► pos -= 3
   └─► Total += terms
                   │
                   ▼
[Return Total = 226754889]
```
