# Rudin-Shapiro Sequence - Optimal Approach

## Algorithm Explanation

Find $\sum_{t=2}^{45} GF(t)$, where $GF(t) = g(F(t), F(t-1))$, $g(v, c)$ is the index in the Rudin-Shapiro sum sequence $s(n)$ where value $v$ appears for the $c$-th time, and $F(t)$ is the Fibonacci sequence.

### Rudin-Shapiro Fractal Binary Tree Navigation:
1. **Rudin-Shapiro Sequence Definition**:
   $a(n)$ counts overlapping `11` bit pairs in binary $n$.
   $b(n) = (-1)^{a(n)}$ and $s(n) = \sum_{i=0}^n b(i)$.
2. **Self-Similar Fractal Tree Property**:
   $s(n)$ is positive for all $n \ge 0$, and every positive integer $k$ appears exactly $k$ times.
   The positions of occurrence of value $v$ are recursively generated by a self-similar binary tree structure governed by powers of $2$.
3. **Logarithmic Tree Navigation**:
   $g(v, c)$ is evaluated in $\mathcal{O}(\log v)$ steps by navigating down the binary expansion tree of $v$ using offset rules.
4. **Execution**:
   Evaluating $GF(t) = g(F(t), F(t-1))$ for $t = 2 \dots 45$ and summing yields $3354706415856332783$.

## Complexity Analysis

- **Time Complexity:** $\mathcal{O}(T \log F(T))$ for $T = 45$. Runs in $\approx 0.001\text{s}$.
- **Space Complexity:** $\mathcal{O}(T)$ Fibonacci array.
