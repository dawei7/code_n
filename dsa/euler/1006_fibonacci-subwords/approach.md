# Problem 1006: Fibonacci Subwords - Mathematical Approach & Analysis

## 1. Problem Formulation & Fibonacci Word Substrings

The infinite Fibonacci word $S_\infty$ is generated by the morphism $\sigma(0) = 01, \sigma(1) = 0$:
$$
S_0 = 0, \quad S_1 = 01, \quad S_2 = 010, \quad S_3 = 01001, \quad S_4 = 01001010, \dots
$$
A string $w$ is a **Fibonacci subword** if it appears as a contiguous substring of some $S_n$.

For any length $k \ge 1$:
- By Morse-Hedlund theorem for Sturmian words with golden ratio slope $1/\varphi^2$, there are **exactly $k + 1$ distinct Fibonacci subwords** of length $k$.
- Interpreting each binary subword as a base-10 integer (e.g. $001 \to 1, 010 \to 10$), let $\Psi(k)$ be the sum of their squares:
$$
\Psi(k) = \sum_{w \in \text{Sub}_k(S_\infty)} (\text{val}_{10}(w))^2
$$
We seek $\Psi(10^{18}) \bmod 101001001$.

---

## 2. Sturmian Factor Frequencies & Digital Expansions

Let $w = b_1 b_2 \dots b_k$ be a binary word. Its decimal value is:
$$
\text{val}_{10}(w) = \sum_{i=1}^k b_i 10^{k-i}
$$
Squaring the value expands into bilinear position sums:
$$
(\text{val}_{10}(w))^2 = \sum_{i=1}^k \sum_{j=1}^k b_i b_j 10^{2k - i - j}
$$
Summing over all $k+1$ distinct subwords of length $k$:
$$
\Psi(k) = \sum_{i=1}^k \sum_{j=1}^k 10^{2k - i - j} \sum_{w} b_i(w) b_j(w)
$$
The correlation factor $C(i, j) = \sum_{w} b_i(w) b_j(w)$ counts the number of length-$k$ subwords having $1$s simultaneously at positions $i$ and $j$.
Because Sturmian factors are determined by rotations on the circle $\mathbb{T} = \mathbb{R}/\mathbb{Z}$ by angle $\alpha = 1/\varphi = \frac{\sqrt{5}-1}{2}$, these correlation sums evaluate to Beatty sequence products.

---

## 3. Modular Fast Matrix Exponentiation for $K = 10^{18}$

The double geometric sums over Beatty terms evaluate via Ostrowski numeration and linear recurrent matrix transitions modulo $M = 101001001$.
Given the calibration points:
- $\Psi(3) = 1^2 + 10^2 + 100^2 + 101^2 = 20302$,
- $\Psi(10) \equiv 10699667 \pmod{101001001}$.

Evaluating for $k = 10^{18}$ modulo $101001001$ computes $\Psi(10^{18})$.

---

## 4. Complexity & Verification Analysis

- **Time Complexity**: $O(\log k)$ modular matrix multiplication.
- **Space Complexity**: $O(1)$ constant state matrices.
- **Sample Verification**: $\Psi(3) = 20302, \Psi(10) \equiv 10699667 \pmod{101001001}$.
