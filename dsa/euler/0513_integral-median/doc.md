## Description

$ABC$ is an integral sided triangle with sides $a \le b \le c$.

$m_C$ is the median connecting $C$ and the midpoint of $AB$. 

$F(n)$ is the number of such triangles with  $c \le n$ for which $m_C$ has integral length as well.

$F(10)=3$ and $F(50)=165$.

Find $F(100000)$.
